Why Us

Dance School for all

Home/Why Us

rumPUREE is Thailand's top dance school for all ages and skill levels.

Over 30 Dance Styles, 120+ Weekly Classes

You can explore the art and culture of dancing from around the world at rumPUREE. There are over 30 dance styles and more than 120 weekly classes to choose from.

Thailand's Top Faculty

Our faculty team is composed of talented dance professionals who have extensive experiences in their dance styles. They can guarantee that if you can walk, you can dance well as well.

Convenient Locations

Our both studios are located in the business area with easy access by both private cars and public transportation.

Friendly and Welcoming Community

rumPUREE is more than just a dance school. Many students even say it is their second home. Our aim is to create a community of people with passion for dancing with a friendly atmosphere, full of positive energy and welcoming everyone, whether you're dancing for the first time or for ten years.

Amazing Team and Studio

The school has a professional team to assist you in reaching the world of dance with ease. We also have dance studios and sound equipment that meet international standards, comparable to the classes in New York or London.
