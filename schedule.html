Level of Dance

Find the right level for your journey

Introduction (INTRO)

This level is suggested for an absolute beginner with no previous dance experience.

All Level (ALL)

This level is recommended for people who have danced for at least 3 months. Instructor will adjust class content to fit participants in the class.

Beginner (BEG - LEVEL 1)

This level is designed for people who are brand new to dance. It is strongly recommended to start on the suggested date and continue to complete the whole cycle in order to build a good foundation.

Beginner Intermediate (BEG INT - LEVEL 2)

This level is designed for people who have passed beginner or have danced for at least 6 months. Students should be able to do all basic movements in the beginner class. Please ask for instructor's permission before attending the class.

Intermediate (INT - LEVEL 3)

This level is designed for people who have passed beginner and advanced beginner levels or have danced for at least 12 months. Please ask for instructor's permission before attending the class.

Intermediate Advanced (INT ADV - LEVEL 4)

Permission from instructors only.

Advanced (ADV - LEVEL 5)

Permission from instructors only.

Choreography (CHOREO)

Students who would like to take a Choreography class need permission from the instructor. Participants are also required to take a technique class at least once a week.
